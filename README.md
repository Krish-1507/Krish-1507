## How I approach AI systems

I do not treat the LLM as the entire product.

A useful agent also needs:

**Context**
The system needs to understand the environment it is operating in.

**Tools**
The model needs controlled ways to interact with software and external systems.

**State**
Long-running work needs memory, persistence, and recoverable workflows.

**Boundaries**
Agents should know what they can change, what they cannot change, and when human approval is required.

**Verification**
A successful tool call or passing test does not automatically mean the task was completed correctly.

This is the part of AI engineering I spend most of my time exploring.
